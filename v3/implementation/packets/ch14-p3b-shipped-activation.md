### The per-class rules the two new steps must satisfy, IN FULL

C2, C3, C4, C5 and C6 are the authorities; their rule sets are pulled here
rather than pointed at, because a keyset without its presence rules and
its refusals under-specifies the shape [R-DELEGATION-CLOSURE]. This block

- **`humanGate` (C2)** — keyset CLOSED, exactly `{ type, role, instruction,
  decisions }`, and all three of `role`, `instruction`, `decisions` are
  REQUIRED. `transitions`, `gates`, `agentConfig` and `recommends` are
  REFUSED on this class. The ADMITTED form additionally carries the
  admission-produced channel-direct fields (`advancesRound`, the ch13v2
  produced ref positions) — producer-owned, never authorable, OUTSIDE the
  keyset by the standing rule, which is why they are not a violation of
  the closed keyset and why T7's comparison meets them.
- **`wait` (C3)** — keyset CLOSED, exactly `{ type, wait, onResume }`; no
  `role`, no `instruction`. `wait` REQUIRED, itself a CLOSED map
  `{ kind, resumeEvents }` with BOTH fields REQUIRED: `kind` a nonempty
  id-grammar string — C10's integer ban included — that is not a member of
  the kernel-owned wait-kind set ch12-C23 names; `resumeEvents` a NONEMPTY list of UNIQUE event-type ids.
  `onResume` REQUIRED, an open map event-type → target whose keys must be
  MEMBERS of `resumeEvents`, whose VALUES lie in the `steps ∪ terminal`
  domain (its own declared lane, with its own refusal — the domain here is
  C3's, not C4's, which governs a `decisions` entry's `target`), and where
  a `resumeEvents` member with no route is admissible — INCLUDING the
  degenerate all-members-routeless empty map, which C3 makes one uniform
  rule rather than an empty special case.
- **`decisions` (C4)** — an open map, ≥1 decision REQUIRED (an empty map is
  the model's `decision_gate_empty`); each entry a CLOSED map
  `{ target, payload? }`; any other entry key REFUSED. `target` REQUIRED,
  and its two failure routes are DISTINCT rather than one lane: an ABSENT
  `target` is the shape family's missing-key refusal, while a PRESENT
  non-string or unresolvable `target` draws the membership lane's ONE
  `decision_target_unresolved` finding — a decided collapse of the
  non-string case into the membership fault, not an unspecified one. The
  target domain is `steps ∪ terminal`. SELECTOR AUTHORITY is per instance:
  `transitions` is principal-committed by the bound actor, `decisions` by
  the bound operator, `onResume` kernel-classified — which is the contract
  ground for the authority rung existing on the submit path and not on the
  resume path. A route naming a SELF-TARGET is legal and genuinely
  re-arrives. AND C4's DECIDED-HERE grammar rule:
  decision KEYS join the one id-grammar namespace, so C10's integer ban
  binds them — they feed record enumeration and the wait record's resume
  events, and need the same enumeration-stable grammar as every other id
  class.
- **the payload spec (C5)** — `payload` OPTIONAL, a map field-name → spec;
  field names are id-grammar strings, C10's ban included (they are enumerated by `required_fields`); each spec a CLOSED map

  not-required, so `{}` is a legal spec. REFUSED: a non-map spec, an
  unknown spec key, a present non-boolean `required`. AND C5's
  DECIDED-HERE carry rule: payload fields a submitted decision carries
  BEYOND the declared spec are CARRIED as uninterpreted data, never
  refused. That rule is a RUNTIME rule, not an admission rule, so it is
  driven where the runtime is — the seam grid's undeclared-field cell —
  and not by J1.
- **`recommends` (C6)** — an open map event-type → decision key on the

  decision-key grammar, so C10's integer ban binds it — an in-class key
  such as `"10"` is refused there exactly as it is in `decisions`.
  `recommends` is legal on agent steps only.

## The operator-intent seam: site × shape × phase

The two shipped verbs become reachable over the SHIPPED template at every
position a run of it can occupy, and most off-diagonal cells are newly
reachable HERE — so the grid is written ONCE at authoring rather than
discovered lane by lane. Sites are the two verbs. **PHASES are dimension

and `review` collapse into a single ACTIVE phase, because no guard on
either path reads the step id; and TWO positions join them that no declaration of this delta produces
but a shipped run can occupy: `CREATED`, which `create` alone commits,
and WAITING(`kickoff_pending`), which `create --mode deferredKickoff`

rejects it as an unknown option, so the mechanism is spelled across BOTH
verbs. A lane staged on either half alone answers `not_waiting` where
`not_bare_wait` is owed. TERMINAL is likewise MORE THAN ONE position — the disposition domain is
`done | cancelled | failed`, of which a shipped run reaches `done` and
`cancelled` — `failed` has no production writer this template can reach,
